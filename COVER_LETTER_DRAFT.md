Dear Editors,

Please consider our manuscript, “FA MAS Contract Aware Recovery for Stateful Agent Service Workflows,” for the special issue.

The manuscript studies how service contracts constrain recovery of state-changing
agent workflows. It provides a proposed recovery architecture, a reduced
executable controller, finite-action Pareto selection, two local HTTP/SQLite
implementations, and direct checks against pinned public retail and airline tool
code. It reports negative findings, distinguishes historical and corrected
exploratory simulations, and supplies reproducible raw evidence.
Development-only model-provider compatibility checks have been performed but
have not qualified a configuration for held-out analysis; no held-out live-model
or production advantage is claimed. Compensation has no resolved aggregate
benefit in the corrected authored simulator, and failed cleanup remains unsafe.

This work addresses service execution, recovery semantics and terminal-state verification. It does not claim novelty for compensation, persistent state or validation alone, and explicitly compares related transactional-agent systems.
