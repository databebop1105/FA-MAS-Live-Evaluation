# Author confirmation sheet for FA-MAS

These are statements for the author to check against records before final submission. They are **not confirmed** by the research artifact or by the assistant.

1. **Authors and contributions.** Confirm every person's name, affiliation, email, and substantial intellectual contribution; decide the author order with all contributors. Record who conceived the study, designed the protocol, implemented the prototype, analyzed the data, drafted or revised the paper, and approved the final version. Account for any collaborator's actual contribution and obtain each author's consent and accountability. If the current single-name byline is inaccurate, correct it in both manuscript and portal.
2. **Funding and support.** List each grant, sponsor, grant number, institutional resource, funded model credit, and sponsor role, or explicitly state that there was no external funding only if true. Verify whether API credits or compute were provided by any entity.
3. **Competing interests.** Disclose relevant financial and nonfinancial relationships or affirm none only after checking with every author and the portal's questions.
4. **Originality and related work.** Identify prior public preprints, workshop versions, closely related manuscripts, dataset/code releases, and concurrent submissions. Check overlap and permissions; affirm original work and no prohibited concurrent journal submission only if true.
5. **Research and AI assistance.** Verify the manuscript acknowledgment's description of OpenAI ChatGPT and Codex assistance across Sections I–XIII, code, figures, and analysis, and revise it if the actual provenance differs. Personally check every scientific claim, citation, figure, source file, and raw result. Confirm any venue-specific AI-disclosure field.
6. **Submission package.** Confirm the special-issue selection, current venue instructions, official-template upload rendering, 14-page maximum, source permissions, data/code availability statement, and all portal declarations.

Record for each item: confirmed by whom, date, required correction, and supporting record. Do not sign or submit these statements with placeholders or unchecked assumptions.
