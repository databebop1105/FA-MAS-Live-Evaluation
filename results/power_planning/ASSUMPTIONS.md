# Live-bank power planning assumptions

These are hypothetical inputs, not estimates from live or held-out observations. The paired effect takes values -1, 0 and +1, with assumed discordance q and mean delta. Four task-variant/model pairs share each of 16 service/fault clusters. The assumed intracluster correlation is rho. Standard-error variance is (q-delta²)(1+3rho)/64. A noncentral-t approximation with 15 degrees of freedom provides scenario planning, not exact small-sample power. It is a reference cluster-mean t-test, not the implemented percentile bootstrap/conditional sign test. The alpha=.05/3 rows illustrate a conservative multiplicity threshold, not exact Holm power. Blank MDE means 80% power was not reached within delta<=q under this approximation. Unknown discordance/ICC, authored clusters and distribution shape make actual power unresolved. Results must not justify claims of adequate power, real effect sizes, external validity or a favorable acceptance probability. Larger independent task families should be planned before freezing if modest effects matter.
